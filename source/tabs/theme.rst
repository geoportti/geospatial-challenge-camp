Sustainable Natural Resources Management and Monitoring
============================================================

Natural resources, from forests and water to minerals and agricultural land, are the foundation of life and economies. 
Yet, their unsustainable use, climate change, and competing demands threaten their availability for future generations.

In 2027, the Geospatial Challenge Camp invites you to tackle real-world challenges in sustainable natural resources 
management and monitoring. Using geospatial data and technologies, you’ll collaborate with experts, organizations, 
and peers to develop innovative solutions that balance the sustainability aspects of natural resources management and 
monitoring.

Why is this theme timely?
---------------------------
Natural resources are under unprecedented pressure:

- Forests are shrinking, yet they are critical for biodiversity, carbon storage, and livelihoods.
- Water resources are over-exploited, with droughts and pollution threatening access for millions.
- Soils are degrading, reducing agricultural productivity and increasing food insecurity.
- Minerals and raw materials are in high demand, but extraction often comes at an environmental and social cost.

Possible application areas of the theme include:
----------------------------------------------------
- **Forestry:** Illegal logging detection, biodiversity monitoring, carbon stock assessment, and wildfire risk mapping.
- **Water Resources:** Drought prediction, water quality tracking, flood risk analysis, and watershed management.
- **Agriculture:** Precision farming, soil health monitoring, crop yield prediction, and sustainable land-use planning.
- **Minerals & Raw Materials:** Mining impact assessment, circular economy tracking, and responsible sourcing verification.
- **Urban & Coastal Zones:** Green infrastructure planning, coastal erosion monitoring, and urban heat island mitigation.
- **Biodiversity & Conservation:** Habitat fragmentation analysis, species distribution modeling, and protected area management.
- **Climate Action:** Greenhouse gas emission tracking, climate resilience planning, and renewable energy siting.
- **Policy & Governance:** Land-use policy evaluation, indigenous land rights mapping, and cross-border resource management.
