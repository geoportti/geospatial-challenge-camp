Full schedule
===============

The Challenge Camp runs from February to May and includes three on-site events:

- **Kick-off: 22–23 February 2027**, Urban3, Maria 01, Helsinki
- **Midterm event: 17-18 March 2027**, University of Turku, Turku
- **Final event: 11 May 2027**,  Main Building, University of Helsinki, Helsinki

In addition to the on-site events, participants will have **weekly online mentoring sessions on Mondays from 
14:00 to 16:00**, starting once groups have been formed and begin working on their challenges. These online sessions provide 
guidance, feedback, and collaboration opportunities, complementing the independent work required throughout the course.

Timetable and co-creation process
-------

The course runs for 12 weeks with the following phases and structure:

.. list-table::
    :widths: 3 8
    :header-rows: 1
    :stub-columns: 1
    :align: left

    * - Weekly timeline
      - Details

    * - Kick-off week 1
      - **Kick-off**:
          * **22-23 February 2027 Kick-off** (on-site, 2 full days)
          * A half a day seminar with all the participants and collaborators
          * A 1,5-day workshop with the participants and key mentors
          * Introduction to the challenge theme, meeting and networking with stakeholders, forming teams
    
    * - Week 2
      - **Challenge introduction**:
          * Getting familiar with the context of the challenges
          * Q&A with challenge owners (online)

    * - Weeks 3-4
      - **Challenge specification**:
          * Exploring the broad challenge, getting familiar with data and tools
          * Defining each team’s focus/problem, gathering resources and contacting stakeholders
          * Online mentoring
          * **17-18 March 2027 Midterm event** in Turku (on-site, 2 full days)


    * - Weeks 5-6
      - **Ideation/prototyping**:
          * Rapid ideation of solutions, testing data and tools
          * Presentation of the solution ideas and feedback

    * - Weeks 7-9
      - **Solution building**:
          * Teams work at their own pace
          * Online mentoring for support and check-ins
          * Practice pitching

    * - Weeks 10-12
      - **Finalization**:
          * Preparing final outputs
          * **11 May 2027 Final event in helsinki** (on-site, half a day)
                * Organized as part of the `Geoinformatics Research Days conference <https://geoinformaticsdays.geoportti.fi/en/latest/>`_ (10-12 May, 2027). All GCC participants are encouraged to attend the full conference. Participation is free of charge.
          * Groups present their solutions to the rest of the course, stakeholders, and public audience
